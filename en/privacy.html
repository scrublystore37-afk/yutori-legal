Privacy Policy

Effective: 4 October 2026 (English version published 7 November 2026)

This policy explains how the Wallog app ("the app") handles your information.

1. Information we collect

  • Accounts, balances, records, recurring rules, goals and nickname that you enter
  • Amount, merchant name and card name received through Apple Pay auto-logging (Shortcuts)
  • If you sign in with Apple or Google, that account's identifier and email address
  • The type of actions you take in the app (for example "added a record" or "opened analysis"), and the app and device type
  • Your paid plan purchase status

We never receive your bank or card login IDs or passwords, or your card numbers.

2. How we use it

We use this information only to calculate and show your balances and net worth, sync and restore your records, provide the paid plan, and improve the app. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. Usage analytics

To improve the app, we send PostHog the type of action, the app and device type, and your language. We never send what you enter, such as account names, merchant names, amounts or notes. We do not store IP addresses and do not record your screen.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (sign-in and data storage, Tokyo region)
  • Firebase App Check (protection against unauthorised access)
  • RevenueCat (managing paid plan purchase status)
  • PostHog (usage analytics)

To get fund prices, the app may query publicly available information from fund managers. No personal information is sent when it does.

5. Storage and security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and is set up so that only your own account can read and write it.

6. Deletion

You can delete your data and account at any time from "Account" in the app. Deleted data cannot be recovered.

7. Payments

Paid plan payments are processed by Apple. The developer never receives your card details.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will let you know on this page and in the app.
