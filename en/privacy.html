Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains how the Wallog app ("the app") handles your information.

1. Information the app keeps

The accounts, balances, records, recurring rules, goals and nickname you enter, and the amount, merchant name and card name received through Apple Pay auto-logging (Shortcuts). There is no sign-up or login, and we do not collect account details such as your email address or name. We never receive your bank or card login IDs or passwords, or your card numbers.

2. How it is used

This information is used only to calculate and show your balances and net worth, and to sync and restore your records. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. Where it is stored

Your data is stored on your iPhone and in your own iCloud (Apple's CloudKit private database). We cannot read it. If iCloud is turned off, it is stored on this iPhone only.

4. Usage analytics

To improve the app, we send PostHog the type of action you take (for example "added a record" or "opened analysis"), the app and device type, and your approximate region. We never send what you enter, such as account names, merchant names, amounts or notes. We do not store IP addresses and do not record your screen.

5. Payments

We use RevenueCat to check paid plan purchases. Only the purchase record and a random ID assigned by the app are sent. Payments are processed by Apple, and we never receive your card details.

6. Third-party services

Apple iCloud (data storage), PostHog (usage analytics) and RevenueCat (purchase management). To get fund prices, the app may query publicly available information from fund managers; no personal information is sent. Only when carrying over data from an earlier version of the app does it read from the previous storage, Google Firebase.

7. Deletion

You can delete your data at any time from "Account" in the app (it is also removed from iCloud). Deleted data cannot be recovered.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will let you know in the app and on this page.
