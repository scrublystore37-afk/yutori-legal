FAQ

I paid with Apple Pay but nothing was logged
Create the automation in the Shortcuts app from "New Blank Automation" (Transaction), then pass the transaction's amount, merchant and card into Wallog's "Record Payment" action. The app has step-by-step instructions under "Set up Apple Pay auto-record". You can always add or fix a payment by hand.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your Apple ID, then Subscriptions.
